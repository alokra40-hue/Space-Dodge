Privacy Policy

Welcome to Space Dodge. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our game.

The game may use your browser's local storage to save game-related information such as your best score. This information remains on your device and is not used to identify you personally.

Advertising

If advertising is introduced in the future, third-party advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements, subject to their own privacy policies.

Third-Party Services

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing those policies when applicable.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game or its services change.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Space Dodge project owner.
